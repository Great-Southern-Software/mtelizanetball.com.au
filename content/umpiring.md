Umpiring is a brilliant way to stay involved in netball, earn some pocket money, and build skills that last well beyond the court:

- **Confidence and self-belief** from making calls and standing by them.
- **Communication skills** that stay clear, calm and fair under pressure.
- **Relationships** within our club and with clubs across the association.

## Support and pathways

Our umpires are mentored by experienced officials, ensuring fair treatment for all players. There are clear pathways and support structures for anyone aspiring to officiate at any level, from first badge to representative netball.

## New to the rules?

Netball Australia publishes the basic rules of the game, which is a great place to start:

[Rules of netball](https://netball.com.au)
