Training is an important part of developing players' netball skills as well as developing cohesion as a team.

## Where we train

Our teams train at the club courts located behind the Community Centre in **Howard Parker Reserve, Mount Eliza**, and at **Mount Eliza Secondary College**.

## Attendance

- Weekly training is expected for NetSetGO through 17&U age groups.
- A/A Reserve level players (13&U to 17&U) must commit to weekly sessions.
- Please let your coach know in advance if you can't make a session.
- If you have an ongoing scheduling conflict, chat with your coach to work something out.
- Open age groups organise their own training.

## What to bring

- **Correct footwear** — runners or sneakers that give the right amount of support to minimise the risk of injury. Players without proper footwear can't take part, and the club accepts no liability for injuries where incorrect footwear is worn.
- **A full drink bottle** — every session, no exceptions.

Coaches maintain a duty of care at training and appreciate parents' support. In bad weather, your coach will decide whether training goes ahead and let the team know.
