Registration for the **2026 winter season** is officially open!

Whether you're a returning player or brand new to netball, everyone is welcome at MENC — from NetSetGO beginners through to our Open division.

[Register now through NetballConnect](https://registration.netballconnect.com/userRegistration?organisationId=799dc8d1-75f6-465b-98e5-0dffe8ced073&competitionId=afa1cda0-bfde-40cf-9f12-b7c144693684)

## What you need to know

- Saturday games commence **14 March 2026** at Jubilee Park.
- Training starts in **February** at Howard Parker Reserve.
- Check the [age groups and key dates]({=site.url('key-dates')}) to find where you fit.

Questions about registering? Email [netballrego.menc@gmail.com](mailto:netballrego.menc@gmail.com).
